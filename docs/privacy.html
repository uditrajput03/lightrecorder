Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • Effective immediately

1. Commitment to Privacy

LightRecorder was created with a fundamental principle: your screen, your audio, and your recordings are entirely yours. The application does not collect, monitor, log, or transmit any user data or recordings.

2. Zero Data Collection

When you use LightRecorder:

  • No Personal Information: We do not ask for, collect, or store names, emails, IP addresses, or device identifiers.
  • No Telemetry or Analytics: There are no analytics frameworks (Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Firebase, TelemetryDeck, etc.) compiled into LightRecorder.
  • No Cloud Uploads: Recorded video and audio streams are processed locally via Apple ScreenCaptureKit and AVFoundation, written straight to your local disk, and never sent across the internet.
  • No Accounts Required: The app does not feature user accounts, logins, or cloud synchronization.

3. System Permissions

LightRecorder requires specific macOS permissions to function as intended:

  • Screen Recording Permission: Required by macOS to access ScreenCaptureKit APIs to capture displays, selected areas, or window buffers.
  • Microphone Permission (Optional): Required only if you choose to record your voice commentary alongside system audio.
  • Folder Access: LightRecorder requests access to save recordings into your designated local folder (defaulting to ~/Movies/LightRecorder) via security-scoped bookmarks.

4. Open Source Verification

LightRecorder is distributed under the open-source MIT License. You do not need to take our word for it—every single line of code is publicly auditable on our GitHub repository.

5. Contact

If you have any questions or security concerns regarding this privacy policy, feel free to open a discussion or issue on our GitHub repository.
